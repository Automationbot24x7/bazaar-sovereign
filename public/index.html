      Welcome to the bazaar

      You inherit a merchant charter — an NFT that owns its own NEAR wallet. Four rival merchants, each run by Claude, trade saffron, silk, tea and salt against you.

      • Every day suppliers auction stock to the highest bidder and townsfolk buy from the cheapest stall.
      • Watch for rumors — droughts, caravans, festivals. Corner a market before it moves.
      • Merchants who run out of gold go bankrupt. Buy their charter and their whole business — NFT, wallet, stock — becomes yours.

      Profits and losses settle as real NEAR transfers every 5 days. You never need a wallet to play.
