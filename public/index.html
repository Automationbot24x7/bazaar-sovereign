      Welcome to the bazaar

      Rivals will know you by it, and it hangs over your stall. Letters only, up to 24.

      You inherit a merchant charter: an NFT that owns its own NEAR wallet. Four rival merchants, each with its own AI mind, trade saffron, silk, tea and salt against you for 24 days. Money is counted in coins.

      • Rumors warn of droughts, caravans and festivals a day early. Corner a market before it moves.
      • Palace orders pay double. Claim one, then buy up the good to deliver it.
      • Haggle: tap a rival to buy their stock or offer to buy out their whole house.
      • Street cries: shout one line a day. The crowd judges it, and a charming cry pulls more shoppers to your stall for 3 days.
      • Rivals who run out of gold go bankrupt. Seize their charter, or buy out a rival outright, and their stall becomes your vassal: it keeps trading under your strategy and pays you 20% of its profit.

      Win: be the richest house (vassals included) on day 24 — or hold three charters and be crowned Sovereign at once.

      Every 5 days profits, stock and charters settle on NEAR. You never need a wallet to play.
